method 2 • permanent overlay
Google My Maps (Sync to iOS & Android App)
  1. Visit google.com/mymaps → "+ Create a New Map".
  2. Click "Import" → select discovered_routes.kml from your workspace.
  3. Open the Google Maps app on your phone → Saved → Maps → select your map.
