Permanent Google My Maps Cloud Overlay →

Want all 20 roads permanently visible directly inside the official Google Maps app on your iPhone or Android? Import the generated KML file into Google My Maps once, and it syncs to your Google account forever.

  1. Download the KML file: Save discovered_routes.kml below.
  2. Open Google My Maps: Go to google.com/maps/d in your browser and click Create a New Map.
  3. Import Layer: Click the Import button under the untitled layer and drag & drop your KML file.
  4. View in Mobile App: Open the Google Maps app on your phone, tap Saved → Maps, and select your imported map. The twisty roads glow on your map as you drive!
